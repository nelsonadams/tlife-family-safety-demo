# T-Life Family Safety: Executive Demo

An interactive concept demo of a **T-Life** feature that tracks children's location and alerts parents when a child:

- **arrives at or leaves** a saved place such as Home or School (geofences)
- **leaves school during school hours** (urgent alert)
- **gets farther than a set distance** from Home or School
- **goes faster than a set speed** (for example, a teen driver on I-90), plus a drive summary afterward

The parent's T-Life app runs inside a phone mockup. The left panel is the presenter console. The right panel ("Under the hood") shows live telemetry, the rules being checked, and the event stream. Trips follow real Bellevue, WA roads from OpenStreetMap routing. Times and speeds are simulated.

## Run it

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `dist/tlife-family-safety-demo.html` directly. It's a single self-contained file, and only the map tiles need internet.

## Presenting

| Scenario | Who | Alerts it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Morning walk to school (7:44 AM) | Emma, 10, SyncUP KIDS Watch | Left Home, Arrived at School |
| 2. Leaves campus at lunch (11:38 AM) | Emma | Left school during school hours (urgent), more than 1 mi from Home |
| 3. Teen driver on I-90 (3:18 PM) | Jake, 16, iPhone | Left School, speed over 65 mph (urgent), distance, drive summary |

- **Run full story** plays all three scenarios back to back.
- Keys: `Space` play/pause, `1`–`3` pick a scenario, `R` restart, `F` fullscreen.
- Live moment: once Jake is on I-90 in scenario 3, open **Alerts** on the phone and drag the speed limit below his speed. The alert fires right away. In **Places**, you can resize the geofences and watch the map update.
- Best viewed at 1440 px wide or wider. Narrower screens hide the "Under the hood" panel.

## Project layout

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`: the demo (vanilla JS + Leaflet, no build step)
- `data/routes.js`: route geometry and speed profiles, generated by `tools/fetch_routes.py` then `tools/build_routes.py`
- `tools/build_single_file.py`: builds `dist/tlife-family-safety-demo.html`

Concept prototype for an internal demo. All people, devices, and trips are simulated.

# T-Family Insights: Lime Partner Portal (concept)

A B2B concept demo. T-Mobile aggregates and anonymizes the movement of people tracked in **T-Life Family Safety**, then sells it to **Lime** as a feed of propensity scores. The scores show *where* and *when* to stage scooters and e-bikes around Bellevue, WA.

- **Map:** hourly hex cells (~0.15 km²) colored by propensity score, foot traffic, or net flow (trip starts minus ends). The top 10 staging zones are numbered on the map.
- **Time bar:** scrub or play through the day, weekday or weekend. The sparkline shows estimated citywide Lime rides per hour.
- **Stage now:** ranked zones for the selected hour, with recommended scooter/e-bike counts and what drives each score.
- **Daily plan:** time windows (morning commute, lunch, school dismissal, evening commute, and so on), each with a "stage by" time and unit counts. Export it with **Export plan (CSV)**.
- **Zone detail:** click a hex to see its 24-hour weekday vs. weekend profile and a breakdown of its score.
- **API:** the sample REST request and response a partner would call.

## Privacy guardrails (adjustable in the left panel)

- Partners only ever receive aggregates: counts per hex cell per hour.
- **k-anonymity:** any cell-hour with fewer than *k* distinct devices is suppressed (default k = 10).
- **Differential-privacy-style noise:** Laplace noise is added to every count. Lower ε means more noise.
- **Opt-in only.** Under-16 devices (all kids' watches) are always excluded. Teens 16–17 are off by default.

## Propensity model

Each score is a weighted blend, adjustable under "Propensity model weights":

- Addressable short trips (0.3–3.2 mi walk/drive legs, plus first/last-mile legs to Link and transit stations). Scooters favor trips under 1.2 mi and e-bikes favor 1.2–3 mi.
- Foot traffic
- Transit proximity
- Net outflow, i.e. places where vehicles drain and need restocking

Ride estimates scale the panel to Bellevue's population (~152k) and assume Lime captures 1.8% of addressable trips.

## Run it

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080   # open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `dist/tfamily-lime-portal.html`, a single self-contained file. Only the map tiles need internet.

## Project layout

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`: the portal (vanilla JS + Leaflet, no build step)
- `data/insights.js`: aggregated panel data, generated by `tools/build_insights.py` (seeded synthetic population of ~2,200 families)
- `tools/build_single_file.py`: builds `dist/tfamily-lime-portal.html`

The original T-Life Family Safety parent-app demo is on `main`.

Concept prototype for an internal demo. All people, devices, trips, and scores are simulated, and place coordinates are approximate.
